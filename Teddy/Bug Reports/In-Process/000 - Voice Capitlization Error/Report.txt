Subject: New Voice Capitalization Error
Severity: Low
Version: Fenra - worlds-rebuild v0.1.0
Description: When a new voice is created, the casing defaults to lowercase, even if the name is entered with uppercase letters.
To Replicate:
1. Launch Fenra - worlds-rebuild
2. Go to the Voices Tab
3. Go to the Voice Editor Tab
4. Click *New voice...*
5. Enter a voice name with capital letters (see Screenshot 1)
6. Click *OK*

Result: The name appears with the correct letters, but in all lowercase.
